## Christmas Tree Fairy Lights Portrait
- slug: `christmas-tree-fairy-lights-portrait`
- before photo to use: **meera**
- paste this prompt:

```
Use my uploaded photo as the only identity reference. Keep my face, facial features, skin tone, hairstyle and body proportions exactly as they are. Do not beautify, slim, lighten or age my face. Place me standing beside a decorated Christmas tree in a warm living room, wearing a red knitted sweater. I am holding a small wrapped gift and smiling softly at the camera. Fairy lights behind me form soft round bokeh. Warm indoor light on my face, cosy evening mood, shot on a 50mm lens with shallow depth of field. Photorealistic, natural skin texture, no extra people, no text or watermark.
```

- then run: `npx tsx scripts/add-sample.ts christmas-tree-fairy-lights-portrait before.png after.png`

## Golden Hour Walk in a Field
- slug: `couple-golden-hour-field-walk`
- before photo to use: **coupleA**
- paste this prompt:

```
Use the uploaded photo of the two of us as the only identity reference. Keep both faces, skin tones, hairstyles and our height difference exactly as they are. Do not blend, swap or beautify our features. Show us walking hand in hand through a field of tall grass at golden hour, looking at each other and smiling. We are wearing a white dress and a beige linen shirt. The low sun is behind us and gives a warm rim of light on our hair. Candid, natural, shot from the front on an 85mm lens with a soft background. ## Restore an Old Damaged Photo
```
- slug: `restore-old-damaged-family-photo`
- before photo to use: **old**
- paste this prompt:

```
Edit the uploaded photo. Keep every person's face, expression and identity exactly as in the original. Do not redraw or regenerate any face. Restore this old photograph. Remove scratches, dust, stains, fold lines and tears. Correct fading and bring back natural contrast. Keep it black and white. Sharpen gently without making skin look plastic. Keep the original clothing, background, pose and framing. No watermark and no added text.
```

- then run: `npx tsx scripts/add-sample.ts restore-old-damaged-family-photo before.png after.png`
